# Mars 4"

### 4" Unibody Long Range FPV Frame

The **Mars 4** is a **160 mm wheelbase unibody frame** built around one goal: hyper efficiency. It takes the truss-style arm design of the Mars 2 and Mars 3 and stretches it for 4" props on 2S, trading punch for quiet, long-endurance cruising. The reference build carries a 5000 mAh 2S 21700 pack for **40 minutes and 23 km round trip**.

## ✨ Features

- **Unibody 4" frame** for maximum rigidity and reduced vibrations
- **Truss-style reinforced arms**, derived from the Mars 2 and Mars 3
- **Central mounting platform** for clean AIO or stack installation
- **Octa-shape base**, rigid and with enough space for 90° USB-C or Micro-USB ports
- **160 mm wheelbase** tuned for efficiency and quiet flight rather than raw power
- **Elastics hooks** for flexible and lightweight battery mounting (fits 2S 21700)
- **Wide motor protection zones** for better crash resistance
- **Compatible with analog and HD systems** (DJI O4, HDZero, Walksnail, analog)
- **13.9 g frame weight**
- **Step file** for your customization needs

## 🔧 Build Specifications & Recommendations

### **Frame Thickness**

- **3 mm carbon fiber:** extremely rigid and barely any twisting, even with 4" arms, still light for the size.

### **Weight**

- **Frame:** 13.9 g (bare, 3 mm carbon)
- **AUW:** 71 g including GPS, without battery

### **Motor Recommendations**

- **1303, 1303.5, 1404 motors** with a **9 mm bolt circle** (4.5 mm hole radius from center), M2
- Kv range:
  * **2S builds:** 5000-6000 Kv
  * **3S-4S builds:** 3000-4000 Kv
- Reference build: **RCinPOWER GTS V3 1303 6000 Kv on 2S**

### **Propeller Size**

- **4" propellers**, **1.5 mm shaft bore**
Recommended options:
  * HQProp T4X2.5 bi-blade (reference build).
  * Gemfan LR 4024 bi-blade

### **Screw Sizes**

- **Motor screws:** M2 × 6-7 mm (depending on motor base thread depth)
- **AIO / stack mounting:**
  * 25.5 × 25.5 AIO → M2 × 10-18 mm (depending on stack and canopy)

### **3D Print Recommendations**

Material: **TPU (95A)**
Suggested prints:
- Canopy (Happymodel 25.5 mm canopy used on the reference build)
- GPS mount (optional)

## 🏗️ Build Tips

- Order props with a **1.5 mm bore**. The GTS V3 1303 uses a 1.5 mm press-fit prop shaft, and 2 mm bore props will not fit
- Use the frame hooks with elastics rather than a strap. It holds even 2s 21700 packs securely and is super lightweight
- Mount the GPS as far from the VTX and power wiring and wrap the wire with copper foild + electrical tape to get a good satellite fix
- Tighten the stack screw with a nut under the AIO to reduce noise
- Keep the battery centered for better CG, and recheck CG after moving to a heavier pack
- Keep wiring short for better performance and reliability
- For long range cruising, tune for smooth throttle rather than fast response. Most of the endurance comes from the low pitch props and 2S, not from the frame alone
